# GVX Local Outreach

GVX's client-acquisition process for local Louisville businesses. It finds good businesses that deserve a better web presence, shows them something concrete, and asks politely.

## Hard Rules

1. **Nothing is sent without the founder's explicit approval.** Draft only. No email, forms, texts, calls, or social messages go out from this skill.
2. **Previews are labeled.** Every preview site says it is a GVX Digital concept for that business, not their real site, and is not indexed or linked publicly. Never imitate the business's identity to mislead anyone.
3. **Facts only.** Personalize with what is publicly visible (listing, current site, reviews the business has made public). Never invent problems, traffic numbers, or lost revenue.
4. **Cold email must comply with CAN-SPAM:** accurate sender and subject, GVX Digital, LLC's physical address, and a working opt-out. Honor opt-outs immediately.

## Workflow

### 1. Find

Search Google Maps by neighborhood and category (restaurants, delis, barbers, florists, bakeries, repair shops, and similar). Keep businesses with a fair-to-good Google Business Profile (solid reviews, active) but a missing, broken, outdated, or social-only website.

### 2. Qualify

Score each prospect 1-5 on: profile strength, web presence gap, local fit (Louisville area), and how well a visible fix would show. Keep scores and the one-line reason in the Prospect Board record (`GVX-Prospect-Studio`). Drop anything under 3.

### 3. Concept

For qualified prospects only, build a preview: a simple, fast, mobile-first page using the business's own public facts (name, hours, menu or services, photos they published). Follow `gvx-brand-voice` for GVX's own wording and the `copywriting` skill for the page itself.

### 4. Draft the note

Use `cold-email`. Shape:
- Subject that names the business plainly
- One sentence on what you noticed (specific, kind, true)
- One sentence on what you built and the preview link
- One clear, low-pressure ask (a 10-minute call, or "reply and I'll send the editable version")
- Signature with GVX Digital, LLC, address, and opt-out line

Under 120 words. No hype. No fake urgency.

### 5. Approve, then log

Present the draft and preview to the founder. After approval, he sends it from his gvxdigital.com mailbox. Record the send date and set one follow-up for 5-7 business days later. Two follow-ups maximum, then archive the prospect (kept on the board as memory, outreach locked).
