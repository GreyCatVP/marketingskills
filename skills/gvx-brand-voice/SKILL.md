# GVX Digital Brand Voice

Use this skill whenever the words are for **GVX Digital's own marketing**. For a client's marketing, read that client's `.agents/product-marketing.md` instead and do not mix the two.

## Before Writing

1. If `.agents/product-marketing.md` exists, read it. Anything it says overrides this file.
2. Load the `copywriting` skill for craft rules. This skill only supplies the GVX specifics.

## Positioning

GVX Digital, LLC (Louisville, KY) is an **AI-native product studio and creative technology consultancy**. It builds intelligent products and websites where creativity, craftsmanship, and lasting business value matter more than speed alone.

Three doors, in this order:
- **Studio builds**: custom products, sites, and apps built for real use.
- **Growth systems**: SEO, advertising, and MarTech wired to measurable results.
- **Web essentials**: the basics done right (site, listing, email, tracking).

Core claim: **Real builds, not mockups.**

## Voice

- Plain and confident. Short sentences. Say what was built and what it does.
- Craft over hype. No "revolutionary," "cutting-edge," "next-level," "game-changing," "unlock," "supercharge."
- Concrete beats clever. Name the thing: the page, the listing, the workflow, the number.
- Local and personal where true: a Louisville studio, a founder who has shipped software for 25+ years.
- Warm, not salesy. A prospect should feel respected, never cornered.
- Follow the AI-tell rules in `copywriting` (no "it's not X, it's Y" reveals, no "no X, no Y, no Z" lists, no trailing comma clauses).

## Approved Lines (from gvxdigital.com)

- Real builds, not mockups.
- We Design. Intelligence. We Build. What's Next.
- One studio, three clear doors.
- We build better systems.
- Build with GVX.

Reuse these verbatim. Write new lines in the same register: short, declarative, concrete.

## Naming Rules

- The name is **GVX Digital** (plain wordmark). Never "gvx.digital" and never "GVX Digital Agency."
- The domain is gvxdigital.com.
- Describe the company as a "studio" or "consultancy," not an "agency," unless the audience only understands "agency." Then say "studio" once and explain.

## Never Claim

- Client counts, revenue, rankings, awards, testimonials, or case-study results that are not documented in the repo or provided by the user.
- A client's name or logo without the user's confirmation that they approved it.
- Capabilities not yet shipped. If unsure, ask.
- Pricing. GVX bills clients a flat rate for labor, not metered usage. Do not publish rates unless told to.

## Output Checklist

- [ ] Every claim traces to the site, the repo, or something the user said
- [ ] No banned hype words
- [ ] One clear next step (a reply, a call, or "Build with GVX")
- [ ] Name and domain written correctly
